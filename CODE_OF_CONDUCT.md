# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We aim to make collaboration respectful, constructive, inclusive, and focused on learning. Be considerate, welcome different perspectives, give actionable feedback, and accept responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainer, Karkala Shiva Reddy, via GitHub. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and handled proportionately; conflicts should be escalated to the appropriate course channels. Retaliation is not acceptable. This concise team policy is project-specific and does not claim adoption of an external code-of-conduct license.
